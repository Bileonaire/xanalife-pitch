## 📖 RECOMMENDED MEETING APPROACH

### Before the Meeting:
1. **Read**: XanaLife_Pitch_Canvas.md thoroughly
2. **Print**: XanaLife_One_Page_Summary.md (or have it ready to email)
3. **Skim**: XanaLife_First_30_Days_Plan.md so you can speak to specifics
4. **Know**: The presentation slides structure (but don't formally present)

### During the Meeting:
1. **Start with listening** - Ask Esther about her vision, challenges, goals
2. **Adapt to her style** - If she's formal, use presentation structure. If casual, conversational canvas.
3. **Reference specific solutions** - "One thing I noticed is you have 32 followers. Here's how we could get to 500..."
4. **Show the 30-day plan** if she asks "What would you actually do?"
5. **Don't overwhelm** - You have lots of ideas, but pick 3-4 key ones to emphasize

### After the Meeting:
1. **Send follow-up email** within 24 hours
2. **Attach One_Page_Summary.md** as reference
3. **Mention 2-3 specific things she said** to show you listened
4. **Propose next steps** - "Shall I create a formal proposal by Friday?"

## 💡 PRO TIPS

### What to Emphasize:
✅ You're a neighbor (local investment)  
✅ 3-in-1 partner (software + video + design)  
✅ Quick wins (show value in 30 days)  
✅ Online ordering (revenue driver)  
✅ Video content (your unique edge)

### What to Downplay:
❌ Don't lead with technical jargon  
❌ Don't oversell features she doesn't need yet  
❌ Don't compare to big brands (stay focused on local)  
❌ Don't mention pricing (you said no pricing today)

### Questions to Definitely Ask:
1. "What's your biggest frustration right now with getting customers?"
2. "If we could solve ONE thing in the next 30 days, what would it be?"
3. "Have you tried digital marketing before? What happened?"
4. "Who is your ideal customer?"
5. "What's your vision for XanaLife in 1 year?"

### If She Asks About Money:
"I want to understand your needs first, then I'll create a proposal with options. We can start small with a pilot project, or go comprehensive. What's most important is that you see value."
